## 🗺️ Dashboard Insights

* *Content Mix:* Platform heavily favors *Movies (4,265)* over *TV Shows (1,969)*.
* *Growth Peak:* Exponential growth boom starting in 2010, peaking sharply between *2017 (682 titles)* and *2018 (646 titles)*.
* *Target Audience:* Dominated by mature content, led by *TV-MA (2,027)* and *TV-14 (1,698)*.
* *Global Footprint:* The *United States (2,032)* is the top producer, with *India (777)* serving as the leading strategic market in Asia.
* *Top Directors:* Led by *Raúl Campos & Jan Suter (18 titles)*, followed by Marcus Raboy (14) and Jay Karas (13).
